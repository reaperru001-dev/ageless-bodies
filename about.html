✦ Our Specialized Areas

Focused Support for Your Health Priorities

Every area of support is approached in the context of your individual health profile, lifestyle, requirements, and goals.

Weight & Fitness

Fat loss: Personalized fat-loss strategies designed around nutritional needs, lifestyle, body composition, and health goals.

Muscle gain: Nutrition and fitness guidance to support muscle-building goals in a considered, individualized manner.

Weight management: Sustainable weight-management planning that considers relevant health factors, habits, and long-term goals.

Lifestyle & Performance

Lifestyle modification: Practical strategies that can support healthier routines and more sustainable daily choices.

Sleep and circadian rhythm support: Lifestyle and nutritional considerations that may support restorative sleep and healthy daily rhythms.

Cardiovascular workout planning: Cardio planning aligned with current fitness, lifestyle, and personal goals.

Weight and strength-training schedules: Training schedules designed to complement nutrition, recovery, and fitness objectives.

Metabolic & Medical Nutrition

Diabetes: Nutritional support for Type 1 Diabetes and Type 2 Diabetes management; in early-stage Type 2 Diabetes, remission-focused strategies may be considered where clinically appropriate.

Hormonal health: Nutrition and lifestyle guidance for hormonal imbalances, including hypothyroidism, hyperthyroidism, and PCOS, as appropriate.

Cardiometabolic health: Dietary strategies that may support the management of LDL cholesterol, triglycerides, and hypertension.

Gut health: Personalized nutrition considerations for digestive concerns, including Irritable Bowel Syndrome (IBS).

Liver health: Nutritional support for Alcohol-Associated Fatty Liver Disease and Non-Alcoholic Fatty Liver Disease (NAFLD); early-stage improvement or remission may be possible in certain cases, where clinically appropriate.
